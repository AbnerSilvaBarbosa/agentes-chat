# 0080. Channel categories in the sidebar

- Status: Accepted (built: the server, the shared client, the web and the phone app). Amended 2026-10-30: §6 and §7 (channels take an order in a workspace with no categories too), and §6 again (a category with no channel someone can see doesn't show for them, unless they manage channels). Amended 2026-11-16: §7 (the phone's Conversas no longer groups conversations: categories are reached from a channel's Categoria screen) Amended 2026-11-14: §8 (agents create categories and place new channels in them)
- Date: 2026-10-30
- Code: `packages/engine/src/channel-categories.ts`, `packages/engine/src/channels.ts` (`createChannel`, `listChannels`), `packages/db/src/schema/chat.ts` (`channel_categories`, `channels.category_id`, `channels.position`), `apps/server/src/routes/app/channels.ts`, `apps/web/src/components/Sidebar.tsx`, `apps/web/src/components/ChannelCategoryDialogs.tsx`, `apps/mobile/src/features/categories/*`

## Context

With a dozen channels, one alphabetical "Canais" list mixes every team's channels. People asked to group them: named sections in the sidebar that open and close.

## Decision

1. **The organization's layout.** A category belongs to the organization (`channel_categories`, with `org_id`) and everyone sees the same sidebar. Its name is kept as typed, at most 40 characters, unique per organization ignoring case (the engine checks the case; the table's unique key is the name as typed). Opening and closing a category is each person's own, in their browser, like the sidebar's other sections.
2. **Who arranges it.** Admins (`configure`) create, rename, reorder and delete categories. Whoever may change a channel (its creator or an admin, as for renaming it) puts it in a category or moves it. Whoever creates a channel may put it in an existing category as they create it.
3. **Order.** `channel_categories.position` orders the categories; `channels.position` orders the channels inside theirs, then by name. Channels with no category (`category_id` null) come first, with no header. Moves renumber the whole category in one transaction (`placeChannel`, `placeChannelCategory`). A group nobody arranged by hand has every position at 0 and reads by name, so a new channel joins it at 0 and the list stays alphabetical, as before categories; once arranged, a new channel goes to its end.
4. **Deleting a category** moves its channels to the end of the ones with none, in their order. Archiving a channel leaves its place as it is.
5. **Live.** Every change sends an org broadcast `channels.layout` with no names in it (private channels stay private); clients refetch the channel list and the categories.
6. **The sidebar.** A category is a section like Agentes: same header, chevron and a + to create a channel in it. Closed, it still shows the open channel and the unread ones. Channels move from their right-click menu ("Mover para…"), the channel details (Categoria) or by dragging. *Amended 2026-10-30:* dragging works with no categories too. In a workspace that has none, the channels drag above or below each other inside "Canais", for the same people, and land through the same `placeChannel` (category null). Before, only a workspace with categories could drag, and with none the list stayed by name. *Amended 2026-10-30 (what each person sees):* someone who doesn't manage channels sees a category only while it holds a channel they can see (the public ones and the private ones they are in, as `listChannels` gives them). A category of private channels they're not in no longer shows them an empty header, and with no category left their channels show under "Canais", as in a workspace with none. Who manages channels (`manage_channels`, ADR 0085) sees every category, empty ones too, to drop channels in. The places that pick a category ("Mover para…", the channel details, Criar canal) still list all of them. The phone's Conversas already leaves out a section with no rows. *Amended 2026-10-31 (the web's look):* a category no longer shares Agentes' header. Lines set the sidebar's parts apart (the tools, the channels, the agents, the people), and Agentes, Mensagens diretas and Canais (with no categories) are titled in small capitals with their chevron after the title, so none reads as a category. A category keeps its header, and its channels line up with the ones in none: every row's mark (an icon, a channel's "#", a face, a category's chevron) sits in one column and every name in the next. A channel's "#" has the chevron's size and ink.
7. **The phone.** Conversas shows the same layout: Canais (no category), one section per category in order, each its five latest and "Ver todos", a closed category keeping its unread channels. The channel's ⋯ sheet and a long press on its row open "Categoria", a pushed screen to pick one; a long press on a category's header gives admins Rename, Categories and New category, all pushed screens. The order moves one step at a time (Move up, Move down): the app has no drag-to-reorder list. *Amended 2026-10-30:* the channel's "Categoria" screen also has its place in the list ("Lugar na lista"): Move up and Move down among the channels of its category, or the ones with none, and where it sits ("2º de 5."), so the phone orders channels with no categories too. The home still lists each section's channels by latest activity, so that order shows in the web's sidebar. *Amended 2026-11-16:* the phone's Conversas lists every conversation by its latest activity with no grouping (Recentes), under a strip of what the person pinned, so it has no category sections, "Ver todos" per category or header long press any more. A channel's category is still picked from its ⋯ sheet and a long press on its row ("Categoria"), and that screen gives admins "Nova categoria" and "Categorias" (the order, rename and delete): one tap from where a channel is placed, without a section on the home to hold the menu.
8. **Agents create categories.** *Amended 2026-11-14.* An agent with the setup tools makes a category, and starts a channel inside one, for the person who asked, with that person's rights and an Undo, as `create_channel` already does (ADR 0061 §8).
   - **Who.** The rights are the code's, not §2's `configure`: a category needs `manage_channels` (ADR 0085, `createChannelCategory`), a channel `create_channels`. The tools check `manage_channels` before writing anything, so a person who may only create channels gets a refusal and no channel when its category is new, and may still use a category that exists. A run with no person behind it (a routine) is refused both tools (`ELSEWHERE` in `tools/run-policy.ts`). `channels` on the category tool is resolved through `listChannels` for that person: a private channel they cannot see is "not there", never named or moved.
   - **The tools.** `create_channel_category` (`name`, optional `channels` to take along, in the order given) refuses a name that exists ignoring case, naming the stored one, and an empty or over-40 name. `create_channel` takes `category`, a name: found, the channel goes at its end (`endOfCategory`, §3); missing, the category is made first in the same transaction as the channel and its receipt, so a channel that cannot be made leaves no category behind. A parallel call of the same run that lost the race to make it reuses it. Both send the `channels.layout` broadcast (§5) once, after the commit (`holdEvents`, `announceLayout`). Code: `packages/engine/src/setup-tools.ts`, `channel-categories.ts` (`findCategoryByName`, `announceLayout`).
   - **Receipts.** A category alone is one `channel_category.created` receipt (`params.name`, `params.channels`). A channel made with its new category is ONE `channel.created` receipt (`params.category`, `params.categoryCreated`), so there is one Undo; a channel placed in an existing category carries `params.category` only. Without `category` the receipt is what it was. The apps say them in `packages/client/src/lib/actions.ts`: channels of one category read as one row naming it.
   - **Undo and redo** (`packages/engine/src/agent-undo.ts`). Undoing a category deletes it and its channels fall back to none (§4); a category already deleted answers "gone" and changes nothing else; redo makes it again by name with the channels that are still live and in none, and `changed since` when the name was taken by hand meanwhile. Undoing a channel made with its category archives the channel and deletes the category when no other live channel is in it (a sibling keeps it; "Desfazer tudo" runs newest first, so the category goes with the last), all checks before any write; a category already gone does not stop the archive. Redo brings back channel and category. An archived channel keeps its place in its category (§4).
   - **Caps per run.** At most 3 categories (made by either tool) and 10 channels. This raises the channels cap from 5 to 10, so one request can create a client's channels in one turn; undone receipts still count.
